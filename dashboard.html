play_circle Module 1

Introduction to xG

Understand Expected Goals and how AI calculates scoring probability.

Expected Goals (xG) is a statistical metric used to evaluate the quality of a scoring opportunity. It calculates the likelihood that a particular shot will result in a goal based on historical data.

Our AI model considers multiple factors: distance to goal, angle, type of assist, whether it was a header or shot, and the positions of defenders and the goalkeeper.

Quiz: Which factor most significantly increases xG?

play_circle Module 2

Pass Networks Explained

How graph theory reveals team structure and tactical patterns.

Passing networks visualize how players interact with each other. Nodes represent players (sized by possession), and edges represent passes between them (thickness denotes volume).

AI identifies clusters, central players (hubs), and structural weaknesses in a team's build-up play, allowing coaches to target pressing triggers effectively.

Quiz: In a pass network, what does a large node typically indicate?

play_circle Module 3

Pressing Metrics & PPDA

Passes Per Defensive Action and pressing intensity.

PPDA measures pressing intensity by dividing passes allowed by the defending team's defensive actions in their attacking regions.

A lower PPDA indicates a more intense press. Modern AI systems also evaluate pressing efficiency—how often a press leads to a high-value turnover.

Quiz: Does a lower PPDA value mean higher or lower pressing intensity?

play_circle Module 4

Injury Prediction Models

Machine learning for muscle injury prevention.

By monitoring GPS data, sleep patterns, and biometric responses, AI models flag players in the "red zone" for potential muscle strains.

This allows managers to rotate squads optimally during dense tournament schedules like the World Cup.

Quiz: Which data source is most commonly used for load monitoring?

play_circle Module 5

VAR Technology & Semi-Automated Offside

How sensors and camera arrays assist referees.

Semi-Automated Offside Technology (SAOT) uses 12 dedicated tracking cameras underneath the stadium roof to track the ball and up to 29 data points of each individual player.

This system generates a 3D animation within seconds, significantly reducing VAR review times and increasing accuracy.

Quiz: How many data points per player does SAOT track?

play_circle Module 6

AI in the Transfer Market

Scouting players globally using machine learning algorithms.

Clubs use AI to analyze thousands of leagues simultaneously, finding undervalued players whose statistical profiles match specific tactical requirements.

Models can project a player's adaptation to a new league based on historical transition data.

Quiz: What is the main benefit of AI in scouting?
