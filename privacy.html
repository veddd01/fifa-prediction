Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to how we build and operate Futora. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

1. Data We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted password. If you sign in via Google or Microsoft, we receive your profile name, email, and profile picture from those providers.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with Futora — pages visited, features used, predictions viewed, and chat conversations with our AI assistant. This helps us improve the platform.

Device Information: We automatically collect your browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and IP address for security and analytics purposes.

Preferences: Your notification settings, language preferences, favorite teams, and dark mode preference are stored to personalize your experience.

analytics

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to: deliver and personalize the Futora platform; generate AI predictions and insights tailored to your interests; send match alerts and prediction updates (with your consent); improve our AI models and prediction accuracy; ensure platform security and prevent fraud.

We never sell your personal data to third parties for advertising purposes.

share

3. Data Sharing

AI Service Providers: Chat conversations are sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. These are not stored by Google beyond the request lifecycle.

Analytics: We use anonymized, aggregated data to understand platform usage patterns. This data cannot identify individual users.

Legal Requirements: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or to protect Futora's legal rights.

cookie

4. Cookies & Tracking

Futora uses localStorage and sessionStorage (browser-based storage) to remember your login state, preferences, and settings. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

The platform loads Google Fonts and the Tailwind CSS framework from CDNs, which may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

psychology

5. AI & Machine Learning

Our AI prediction models are trained on publicly available historical football data (match results, player statistics, tournament records). Your personal data is not used to train AI models unless you explicitly opt in via the Data Sharing toggle in Settings.

If you opt in, only anonymized usage patterns (e.g., which prediction features are most useful) are used — never personal identifiers.

lock

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS), secure password hashing, and regular security audits. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your personal data; correct inaccurate information; delete your account and all associated data (via Settings > Danger Zone); export your data in a portable format; opt out of data sharing for AI improvement.

8. Children's Privacy

Futora is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes via email or an in-app notification. Continued use of Futora after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
